The Comfy Gambit privacy policy

Last updated · 11 November 2026

This policy covers The Comfy Gambit, the Android chess game developed by Shiro Games. The game is being prepared for public release on Android; availability may initially be limited to testers. Its intended audience is ages 13 and older. For questions about your information, email abdmaher01@gmail.com.

For screenshots, features, and game information, visit the official The Comfy Gambit game page.

Your chess progress

The game saves campaign results, unfinished positions, Daily Match records, hearts, hints and preferences on your device. These records let you resume play, track completed puzzles and remember your settings. The campaign and Daily Match can be played offline. These local saves are not uploaded to a Shiro Games cloud-save service or to Neon Chambers’ Unity Cloud Save. Clearing app data or reinstalling without a device backup may remove local progress; restoring a Premium purchase does not restore lost puzzle progress.

Daily Match community leaderboard (player-submitted scores)

When connected to the internet, the game can read the community leaderboard and automatically submit pending Daily Match results to Shiro Games’ Cloudflare-hosted service, including when you have not opened the rankings panel. The service is not required to play: campaign, Daily Match and local score records work offline, and an unavailable API does not prevent play. There is no separate in-game opt-in switch for completed-score synchronization.

To distinguish players, the leaderboard issues a random guest identifier and access token saved on your device. The service stores the guest identifier, a protected hash of the token, match date, board identifier, Master and Correct move counts, awarded score, move count and game outcome in Cloudflare D1. The public leaderboard displays a short guest identifier, score, result and date, not your real name. IP addresses and diagnostic connection data may be processed by Cloudflare to deliver and secure the service. Scores are player-submitted and are not independently verified against complete chess replays; do not treat rankings as official competitive results.

Online guest rankings are retained until deletion or a future service retention policy is established. Clearing the game’s data removes your local token and scores but does not automatically remove Cloudflare records. Contact Shiro Games at the address below to request online score deletion; include your short public guest ID, if available. The service may require additional information to match a record safely.

Optional advertising and Premium purchases

The Comfy Gambit does not display automatic interstitial or banner ads. It offers optional rewarded advertising that you choose to watch in return for in-game benefits such as hearts, hints or takebacks, subject to ad availability. The Android app uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with Unity Ads and Google AdMob; the networks may not always provide an ad. No in-game reward is granted unless a rewarded ad is completed successfully.

Advertising partners and SDKs may collect or receive IP address and IP-derived approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions (including rewarded-video views), diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals to deliver and measure ads and protect their services. Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, Google AdMob and their service providers handle this information according to their own privacy notices. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable ad-consent choices. Depending on applicable law and your choices, ads may be limited or contextual. You can also manage the Android advertising identifier from device settings.

Premium is a one-time, non-consumable optional digital purchase, subject to availability through Google Play. The Premium option is offered through Google Play where available; purchase verification and entitlement restoration have been tested in closed testing. Store availability may vary by region, account or service status. When Premium is offered, Google Play and Unity In-App Purchasing process product IDs, purchase history, receipts, entitlement status, relevant pseudonymous identifiers and SDK diagnostics to verify purchases, prevent fraud and restore ownership. Shiro Games does not receive your payment-card number or banking details. Purchase-related records held by Google or Unity are governed by those providers' retention and deletion processes.

Read the Unity player privacy policy, Google privacy policy, and Cloudflare privacy policy for provider practices.

Optional Android notifications

If you allow Android notification permission, The Comfy Gambit can schedule local reminders on your device for a new Daily Match, available daily credits, and an unfinished Daily Match nearing its UTC expiration. You can deny or later disable these reminders in Android Settings without losing access to the game. These reminders are scheduled by the app on your device, not by a Shiro Games push-notification account or remote marketing list.

Support and retention

If you contact Shiro Games, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you send. We use them to respond and troubleshoot. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the issue. Records may also be retained where required by law or needed to resolve a dispute.

Your choices and deletion requests

To erase the game’s local save, open Android Settings, select The Comfy Gambit under Apps, then open Storage and clear the app’s data. This removes local progress. Device backup settings may affect what is restored after a reinstall. Clearing app data does not delete Cloudflare-hosted guest leaderboard records, cancel a Google Play purchase, or erase data retained by SDK providers.

To ask for access, correction or deletion of records held by Shiro Games, email abdmaher01@gmail.com. Tell us what you want removed. We may ask for information needed to locate the record. Clearing local storage does not remove emails or a store provider’s records.

We will update this page as the game and its services change.
