Player support

For help with The Comfy Gambit or Neon Chambers, email abdmaher01@gmail.com. Tell us which game you play so we can find the right answer.

Report a puzzle or a technical problem

Include the puzzle or chamber number, your device model, the game version if known, and what happened after your last move. A screenshot is helpful if you have one.

Purchases

For a purchase issue, name the game and item and describe what appeared in the game. Leave out passwords and payment-card details.

Data and account deletion

Email abdmaher01@gmail.com with the game name and the records you want removed. Include your player ID if available. See your game’s policy for details about device saves, service providers and records that may be retained.
